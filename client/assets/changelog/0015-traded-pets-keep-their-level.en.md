## Fixed

- **A pet handed to another player was reset to level 1** — trading a growth
  pet's summon scroll, or selling it from a personal shop, wiped everything the
  wolf had earned: its level, experience, health and hunger. The scroll now
  carries the pet exactly as it was, so the new owner summons the same wolf you
  raised. Account Storage and the Guild Vault were never affected.
- **Pets change hands as they are** — a pet that fell in battle now arrives at
  its new owner still needing a Grass of Life, instead of quietly coming back to
  life as a fresh level-1 wolf.
- **Guild Vault and a summoned pet** — storing the scroll of a wolf that was
  still out is now refused with a clear message, the way every other storage,
  trade and sale already did. Doing it before could lose the pet's progress.
