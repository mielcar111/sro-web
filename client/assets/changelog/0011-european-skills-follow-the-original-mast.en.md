## Changed

- **Mastery level no longer multiplies European skill damage** — in the original game only certain skills grow with your mastery level, and the European trees are not among them: a Wizard, Warlock, Cleric, Bard, Rogue or Warrior skill deals the damage printed on it, and your mastery level decides which skills and gear you may use rather than multiplying every hit. This server had been applying that multiplier to every learned skill, which at mastery 80 made European attacks roughly 44% stronger than they should be. Magical skills feel the change most; on physical skills only the small flat part of the damage moves, so Warrior and Rogue hits barely change. Wizards are hit hardest here, because the Spirit passives were already corrected in the last update — those still work exactly as before, and are now the real source of a Wizard's power.
- **Chinese skills are untouched** — Bicheon, Heuksal, Pacheon, Fire, Cold and Lightning skills do grow with mastery in the original, and they still do here, at the same rate as before.
- **Normal attacks follow the same rule** — the staff, dark staff and rod normal attacks still gain power from your mastery, as does every Chinese weapon. The harp normal attack no longer does. Sword, axe, dagger and crossbow normal attacks are unaffected either way.
- **One European skill keeps it** — Sprint Assault still grows with Warrior mastery, exactly as the original has it.
