## Changed

- **Pressing another skill interrupts a combo again** — the last update made combos always play out in full, so the skill you pressed next waited for every remaining strike. On the longer chains that meant standing still for over five seconds, and combat felt like skill, pause, skill, pause. Your next skill now starts as soon as the strike that is running finishes. The trade is back too: strikes you interrupt are given up, so letting a chain finish is still worth it — on Chain Spear - Tiger the last strike alone is more than half the combo's damage. Strikes that had already started keep their damage, which is what the last update set out to fix.

## Fixed

- **The ferry could drop you inside a building** — arriving at the Constantinople and Asia Minor harbours sometimes put you on the sand under the pier instead of on the deck. Down there the dock walls were not solid, so you could walk straight into the buildings and get stuck. Every ferry now lands you on the deck.
- **The Samarkand teleport swallowed your clicks** — the town square around the gate could not be walked on and you could not click anything behind it, because the whole square counted as part of the gate. The square is ordinary ground again, and the gate itself still opens when you click the arch.
- **Storage ignored the page you had open** — putting something away while looking at page 2 sent it to page 1, and dropping an item onto a specific empty slot ignored that slot and filled the grid from the top instead. Storage and the guild vault now put items where you aim them. Stacks still merge with matching stacks first.
- **"That isn't possible right now" when equipping** — Chinese and European gear can only be worn by their own race, but the game refused with a message that explained nothing, so a level-42 ring on a level-42 character looked like a level bug. It now says which race can wear the item, the tooltip marks that line in red like every other unmet requirement, and the item stops being offered to a character who can never wear it.
- **Long sessions got slower and hotter** — after several hours the frame rate fell and the machine heated up, worst with several clients open. Three things were piling up in the background and never being cleared: every sound effect left part of itself behind, some monster data was kept after the monster was gone, and each town or zone change held on to the old camera. All three are now cleaned up as they finish.
