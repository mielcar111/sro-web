## Added

- **Constantinople has mines at last** — European miners had nowhere to swing a
  pickaxe: every stone deposit in the world sat in one graveyard outside Jangan.
  Two grounds are now open. The high plateau of the Golden Plain, out west among
  the lions, is bare rock and easy to read from a distance. The eastern plain
  beyond the north-east walls is gentler country, close enough to town for a
  miner who has only just bought a pickaxe.
- **Two new mining grounds around Jangan** — the flat shelf at the Bandit
  Stronghold, west of the city, is the richest rock in the province and the most
  dangerous to work: bandits and archers hold that ground. The Southern Plains
  seams are the quiet option, scattered across the open field a short run from
  the Southern Plains teleport.

## Changed

- **Stone deposits recover in half the time** — a worked-out deposit used to
  leave you standing around, or walking the whole field over again, before it
  came back. It now returns twice as fast. Mining a field with a friend no
  longer means racing each other to the next rock. Trees recover faster too.
