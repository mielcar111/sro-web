## Changed

- **Chinese imbue damage raised** — after the last update many of you reported
  that Chinese characters now hit far too weakly with an imbue. We checked the
  imbue math against the original game's own server, hit for hit: the last
  update's numbers were the original's, and the earlier ones were too high.
  Even so, we are raising the extra damage of Fire, Ice and Lightning imbues on
  every hit to one and a half times the original game's share, as a balance
  choice. Auto attacks and multi-arrow combos regain roughly two thirds of what
  they lost; big single skills such as Devil Arrow were barely affected and
  move less. The physical part of a hit is unchanged.
- **Imbue power follows your weapon mastery** — the mastery bonus on an
  imbue's extra damage now comes from the mastery of the weapon skill you
  swing with (Bicheon, Heuksal or Pacheon), as in the original, instead of the
  imbue's own Fire, Cold or Lightning mastery. If both are at the same level
  nothing changes for you; a weapon mastery above your imbue mastery makes
  imbued hits a little stronger, and the other way round a little weaker.
