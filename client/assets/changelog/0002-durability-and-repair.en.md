## Changed

- **Equipment wears out six times slower** — durability was draining far too
  fast, especially for casters chaining skills (a weapon could break within a
  single hunting session). Wear from attacking, blocking and getting hit is now
  roughly one sixth of what it was, so a weapon lasts several long hunts
  before it needs the blacksmith.
- **Repair prices are now per item, like the original game** — instead of a
  flat 2 gold per durability point, every piece of gear now carries its own
  repair price from the original Silkroad data. Low-degree gear is cheaper to
  fix than before, while high-degree gear costs properly more — an 8th-degree
  staff is a real bill, a 1st-degree sword is pocket change.
- **Broken gear costs extra to restore** — letting a piece hit 0 durability
  now adds the original game's restoration surcharge on top of the normal
  point repair. Repair before it breaks and you skip the surcharge.
- **Prices are easier to read** — gold amounts you type into the bank, guild
  vault, trade, stall, auction and exchange windows now group their digits
  automatically as you type (1,500,000 instead of 1500000), so no more
  counting zeroes. Pasting numbers with dots, commas or spaces works too —
  the amount always stays exactly what you meant.
