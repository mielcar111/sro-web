## Added

- **Growth pets are here** — the classic levelling wolf. Buy a **Grey Wolf
  Summon Scroll** at any town's stable keeper for 2,000,000 gold, or get the
  exclusive **White Wolf** from the Item Mall. Right-click the flute and the
  cub appears at your side, follows you across zones and teleports, and
  fights with you.
- **Your wolf levels up with you** — it earns a share of your hunting
  experience, visibly grows as it levels, and at level 40 evolves from a cub
  into a full-grown **Wolf** (with a new model). At level 80 it carries the
  **Strong Wolf** title.
- **Pet window & mini bar** — the bar above the hotbar shows your wolf's HP
  and hunger at a glance; click it to open the pet window with HP/HGP/XP
  bars, stats, and stance control. **Offensive** wolves join your fights on
  their own, **Defensive** ones only strike back.
- **Direct pet commands** — the pet window has **Attack** and **Follow**
  orders: send your wolf at your selected monster (it doesn't have to be the
  one you're hitting) or call it back to heel. Drag either one onto a hotbar
  slot and command your wolf mid-fight with a single key, without opening
  the window.
- **Pet care items** — stable keepers stock pet **recovery kits** (heal),
  **HGP recovery potions** (feed — a hungry wolf fights at half strength and
  a starved one runs away into its flute), and **Grass of life** (revive a
  fallen wolf).
- **Recovery kits also heal your horse** — and when a wolf and a horse are
  out together, click a companion's card to choose which one your pet
  potions treat. The chosen card is outlined in gold; a card's portrait still
  opens its window. Feed and revival remain wolf-only — a horse has no
  hunger, and grab pets never tire or fall.
- **Your wolf's experience shows in the system log** — every kill reports
  what the wolf earned, and calls out the moment it reaches a new level.
- **Flutes and pet items on the hotbar** — drag a wolf flute or pet potion
  onto either hotbar. Pressing a flute's key summons the wolf, or opens its
  window if it is already out.
- **Flute tooltips track your pet** — hover a wolf flute to see the pet's
  level, experience, HP and hunger, live while it is summoned.

## Changed

- **Pets keep pace properly** — companions now match your speed when they are
  close and put on a burst to close the gap when they fall behind, instead of
  running on their own fixed pace.
- **A wolf and a horse no longer run through each other** — two companions
  now travel side by side instead of stacking on the same spot.
- **Your wolf is quieter** — its idle growl was repeating like a metronome
  because it stood right beside you all session. It now speaks up far less
  often.
