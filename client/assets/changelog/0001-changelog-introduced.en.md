## Added

- **In-game changelog** — this window! It opens once after every update and is
  always available under **Menu → Changelog**. Older updates stay browsable in
  the list on the left.
- **Profession rankings** — the Rankings window has a new Profession tab: see
  the server's top Lumberjacks and Miners.
- **Pets and horses on the hotbar** — drag a pet summon scroll or a horse onto
  the hotbar (the M slot works too) and summon or ride with a single press. A
  press always uses the scroll that is already active, so it never starts a
  second rental by accident.
- **Page tabs in Inventory, Account Storage and Guild Vault** — the page arrows
  are now numbered tabs. While dragging an item, rest on a tab for a moment and
  the page flips under your cursor, or drop the item straight onto a tab to
  send it to the first free slot of that page.

## Changed

- **Elixirs stack** — elixirs now pile up to 50 per slot in your inventory,
  Account Storage and Guild Vault, and deposits merge into an existing pile
  instead of always taking a fresh slot.
- **Heal-over-time skills in the Macro** — regenerating heals such as Recovery
  Division can now go into the Macro's buff list and are kept up automatically
  while hunting. Buffs that only work on other players can no longer be placed
  there.
- From now on, every update we ship comes with notes here, so you can always
  see what changed on your daily login.

## Fixed

- **Six European Warrior skills were uncastable** — Double Stab, Cunning Stab,
  Charge Swing, Triple Swing, Dual Counter and Deadly Counter wrongly demanded
  a knocked-down target; they now cast normally.
- **Bonus damage against downed enemies** — Warrior and Rogue attacks that are
  meant to hit harder against knocked-down enemies deal that extra damage
  again.
- **No more vanishing deposits** — if a deposit into Account Storage, the Guild
  Vault or Item Auctions failed halfway, part of the stack could disappear.
  Failed actions now always return the full amount to your bag.
- **Swapping full stacks** — dropping a stack onto a full stack of the same
  item now swaps the two instead of doing nothing.
- **Withdrawing big piles** — withdrawing a large stack while your bag is
  nearly full now gives you as much as fits instead of failing outright.
