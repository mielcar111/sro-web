## Added

- **Feature requests** — the bug report window now has a second tab for ideas
  and suggestions. Give your idea a short name, explain it in detail, and it
  goes straight to the team. Well-explained requests are the ones that get
  read and built, so the form asks for a real explanation — a couple of words
  won't go through.
- **Chat context on bug reports** — bug reports now include the last few chat
  lines from around the moment you hit send, which makes trade, party and
  whisper-related problems much easier for us to track down.

## Changed

- **A quick check before sending a bug** — the bug form now asks one question
  before it sends: is something actually broken? If what you typed is really a
  suggestion or a balance opinion, one click moves you to the Feature request
  tab instead, and your report reaches the right queue.
