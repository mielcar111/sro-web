## Added

- **Footsteps** — you, other players and monsters now make footstep sounds while moving, timed to the stride so every step lands with the foot instead of on a loop of its own.
- **The ground decides the sound** — walking on Jangan's marble, on grass, on sand, through mud or across snow each has its own footfall, and it changes under you as you cross from one to the next.
- **Footsteps volume** — a new slider in Settings under Sound, so you can set footsteps to taste without touching your other effect sounds.
- **Auto Potion looks after your pet** — two new lines in the Auto Potion window: one keeps your wolf's health up with recovery kits, the other feeds it before it gets hungry. The recovery kit line lets you pick whether it goes to your wolf or your horse.
- **Buy a whole stack at once** — hold Ctrl and click a merchant's goods to buy a full stack straight away, with no quantity window in between.
- **Vigor in the Auto Potion window** — a new line takes a Vigor Recovery Grain or Potion and gives it two separate bars, one for health and one for mana. Switch on either or both: whichever runs low first, it drinks. Handy as an emergency reserve behind your normal potions, or on its own as a mana backstop.
- **Click the sky to keep running** — clicking empty sky or the far horizon now sends you running that way until something stops you, the way it works in the original client. Click the ground, attack, cast, or press Escape to stop; click the sky again to turn.
- **Camera zoom speed** — a new slider in Settings under General sets how far the camera moves per notch of the mouse wheel, from a third of the current speed up to nearly triple.
- **Twenty seconds of safety when you arrive** — logging in, teleporting, stepping through a gate or standing back up after dying now leaves you protected for twenty seconds. Monsters ignore you and your pet, anything already chasing you breaks off, and no one can attack you. You cannot attack during it either, so nobody can use it as a free opening shot. Effects already burning on you still run their course, and the countdown sits in your buff bar so you can watch it go.

## Changed

- **Horses** — the gallop is now tied to the horse's own stride instead of repeating on a fixed timer, so hoofbeats stay in step with the legs at any speed.
- **Your wolf joins in sooner** — on Offensive it now runs at your target the moment you attack or begin casting, instead of waiting for your first hit to land.
- **Levelling up your wolf** — reaching a new level now heals it to full and fills its hunger gauge.
- **Experience rate** — the server's experience multiplier is now 25×, down from 30×. Skill point gain is unchanged.
- **Splitting a stack** — splitting is now Shift+click in your inventory, because Ctrl+click buys a full stack at merchants.
- **You can pull the camera back further** — the furthest zoom-out now sits noticeably further behind your character, for those of you who felt hemmed in by how close it stopped.
- **A Defensive wolf finally stays out of it** — on Defensive your wolf no longer piles in the moment a monster hits you. It still defends itself if something turns on it, and it still attacks anything you order it onto. Switching it to Defensive in the middle of a fight now calls it off there and then, the same as telling it to follow. Your stance choice is also remembered now, so an Offensive wolf stays Offensive after you log back in.
- **Deep water turns you back** — rivers and open sea now have a depth you cannot wade past. Where you could once walk out along the bottom — under the European bridges especially — you are stopped at the water's edge and have to cross on the deck instead.
- **Universal Pills** — now work the way the original game ran them: they treat the six elemental afflictions — Freezing, Frostbite, Electric Shock, Burn, Poison and Zombie — by taking a fixed amount of time off each one, more per grade. Sleep, bleeds, curses and the other severe afflictions are Purification's territory now.
- **Purification Pills** — also back to the original design: one use picks one of your severe afflictions at random and attempts a cure. Mild afflictions are a certain cure, strong ones can resist, and higher-grade pills reliably cure much stronger ones. A failed attempt does not bounce to another affliction.
- **Pill timers split** — Universal Pills now have their own short reuse delay of about a second, Purification Pills their own twenty seconds, and the two no longer block each other.
- **A pill is spent when you swallow it** — even if there was nothing to cure or the cure failed, exactly like the original game. Auto Potion stays careful for you: it only reaches for a pill when there is actually something it can treat.
- **Fire Shield needs a shield — and then keeps burning without it** — casting any Fire Shield rank now requires a shield equipped, exactly like the original. Once it is up, removing or swapping the shield no longer ends it: the flame stays with you.
- **Fire Shield's protection works the original way** — instead of completely blocking weak elemental afflictions and letting strong ones through untouched, it now weakens every freezing, frostbite, shock, burn, poison and zombie effect that lands on you, so they hit softer and run out sooner.
- **Cure skills cure like the original** — Force Cure, Cure Therapy, Innocent and Cure Melody now pick their targets at random up to their count: severe afflictions get a chance-based cure attempt that can fail against strong ones (and a failed attempt does not bounce to another), while elemental afflictions lose a fixed amount of remaining time per grade. Cure Melody's 80% chance now applies per affliction, not once per song. Tooltips rewritten to say what actually happens.
- **Knockback and knockdown work the original way** — the skills that shove or floor a target now use the original's own numbers. Knockbacks land far more often and push the proper distance; knockdowns scale their chance against the target's level, cap at three in four, shove the victim back, and hold them down for as long as the original held them. Monsters that resist one of the two no longer resist both by accident.
- **Skills that cost your own health** — Berserker, Maddening Attack, Crisis Rush, Prick Attack and Howling Shout now measure their cost against your MAXIMUM health rather than what you have left, so the same skill always asks the same price and never becomes uncastable just because you are hurt. The amount actually taken is worked out when the cast begins, and it can never kill you.
- **Damage lands where the blade does** — a hit's damage now appears at the moment the weapon actually connects in its swing, taken from the original's own animation timings, instead of at the instant the swing starts. The two-strike swings — sword and blade, and the European axe and dagger — read as two separate blows again, each with its own strike sound, spaced exactly as the animation places them.
- **European caster weapons draw before they strike** — the crossbow, warlock wand, wizard staff and cleric rod each take their own short moment before the first hit of a normal attack, instead of striking the instant you engage.
- **Protective wards weaken afflictions instead of walling them off** — Holy Word, Poison Circle and the Warrior's Protection used to be all-or-nothing: below their rank nothing landed at all, above it everything landed untouched. They now make every attempt harder, with the protection fading gradually as the affliction outranks the ward.
- **Panic** — no longer delays your health potions. It now does what it did in the original: cuts your maximum health, cuts how much healing you receive, and drains mana every two seconds.
- **Combustion** — likewise. Instead of one instant mana burn plus a potion lock, it cuts your maximum mana, cuts how much mana you recover, and keeps draining while it lasts.
- **Short-sight** — two short-sight effects now add up to one reduction instead of multiplying, and the reduction is no longer floored, matching the original.
- **Dull** — monsters under Dull now also take a quarter longer between actions, on top of the movement and attack slow.
- **Cleric armour passives need the full set** — Faith, Charity, Glory Armour and Favour Armour now require all six armour pieces of the right kind plus a Cleric Rod. One matching piece used to be enough; with a slot empty or a mismatched piece worn, the passive goes quiet until you complete the set.
- **Bard songs and dances survive a fight** — a landed hit no longer ends your performance outright. Each hit gets roughly a one-in-five chance to break it, and Prism and Screen Dance lower that further. Your own attacks and skills no longer end it at all; only starting another performance does — and a dance ends a song, while a song leaves a dance playing.
- **Force Harmony** — its health-recovery and mana-recovery bonuses are now two separate values, as the original authored them.
- **Link skills refuse rather than drop** — Physical and Magical Division, Pain Quota, the Force blessings and the Warlock's damage-over-time channels each have a limit on how many targets you can hold. Casting past the limit is now refused outright instead of silently cutting the oldest link. Link range is also checked properly: if the other end vanishes, the link ends.
- **Reverse Oblation** — now carries its downsides as well as its gifts: for the two minutes it lasts your outgoing damage is halved, and when it runs out your health and mana are capped back down.

## Fixed

- **Elixirs, Lucky Powders and Magic Stones on the ground** — these all dropped as the same plain cloth pouch. Each now lies on the ground in its own container, so you can tell from a distance what fell.
- **Accessories came out of storage with their stats gone** — a ring, necklace or earring put into Account Storage or the Guild Vault could come back with its white stat percentage and its blue stats wiped to nothing. Storage no longer rebuilds your item on the way out, so nothing is lost putting things away. Accessories already emptied this way are being restored to what they were.
- **Avatars that showed up hollow or nearly invisible** — around twenty-five costumes were built in a way that threw away most of the outfit while still hiding the body underneath, leaving a hollow shell or a character who looked barely there. All of them have been rebuilt and now wear properly.
- **Bridges that dropped you, and walls that were not there** — the river bridge at Constantinople had an invisible wall part-way across; the plank docks and the viking bridge put you on the river bed or wedged you underneath the deck; and the high bridge in Donwhang and the ravine bridge in Jangan let you step off the side into the drop. Every one of them now carries you over properly, in both directions.
- **Ground that swallowed you** — the Shakram pit in Hotan could take you in from any side, and the canyon notch in Donwhang was a one-way pocket you could walk into but not climb out of. Neither can trap you now.
- **T-pose, for real this time** — some of you still saw your character or nearby monsters standing stiff with arms straight out after logging in or arriving in town, even after the last patch. Those remaining cases are gone: everyone appears in their proper stance from the first moment, wings and mounts included.
- **Leaving the game in a background tab** — if the game sat unfocused in another tab for a long while, monsters could be invisible when you came back and the game could stay sluggish until you reloaded. The world now keeps itself in order while you're away, and everything is back and moving the moment you return.
- **The swirl under your feet** — the movement-scroll aura could go missing while the scroll was still running, and nothing short of a restart brought it back. Buff effects now notice when they have gone and put themselves back, so what you see matches the buffs you actually have.
- **European skills in German and Turkish** — if you played in German or Turkish, every European skill still showed its name and description in English while the rest of the game was translated. All of them — Warrior, Rogue, Cleric, Wizard, Warlock and Bard — now read in your own language.
- **Tracing another player** — tracing stopped without ever telling you why. Now every stop says so in the system channel: you moved, you attacked, you used a skill, they got too far away, and so on. Tracing also no longer breaks on a click that did nothing, no longer ends the instant you start auto-hunting without a word, and no longer leaves you standing still while it still thinks it is following someone.
- **Stunned characters can use items** — being stunned wrongly blocked potions and pills. As in the original game you can drink through a stun; the stun itself, though, is not something any pill removes.
- **Disease** — Purification Pills now cure Disease, as the original game always did. The old description claiming medicine cannot cure it was wrong and has been corrected.
- **Cure skills** — cure-type skills could remove Stun and Petrification, which the original never allowed, while failing to reach Darkness and Combustion, which it did allow. Their reach now matches the original exactly.
- **A phantom Fire Shield rank** — the Fire mastery tree offered one more Fire Shield rank than the original game ever sold. That unlearnable extra rank is gone; everything you could actually learn is untouched.
- **Knockback distance and chance were swapped** — the shove skills read their two numbers the wrong way round, so a 35% knockback fired half the time and pushed you the wrong distance. Every one of them now behaves as written.
- **Poison lasted slightly too long** — poison's duration had an extra stretch that the original never had. It now runs exactly as long as its strength allows.
- **"That isn't possible right now" when your mana was clearly low** — a potion only takes effect over a few seconds, and while it is still coming through, the next one of the same kind is refused. The game used to answer that with a blank refusal, which read as a bug when your bar was visibly half-empty. It now tells you plainly that you are still absorbing your last potion — and when a bar really is full, it says that instead.
