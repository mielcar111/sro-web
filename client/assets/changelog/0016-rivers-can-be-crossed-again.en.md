## Fixed

- **The Samarkand river blocked the whole province** — the crossing toward
  Taklamakan, and the canal by the rock golems in the south, stopped you dead at
  the water's edge no matter which bank you started from. Shallow water is
  wadeable again everywhere: you walk in, the water comes up to your chest, and
  you come out the other side. Deep water — the open sea and the Constantinople
  canal — still turns you back at the shoreline.
- **Speed scrolls no longer vanish when you log out** — a Scroll of Movement
  used to disappear the moment you left the world, even with most of its hour
  left. The remaining time now waits for you: log back in and the buff is still
  there, with exactly the time it had when you left. Dying still ends it, as
  before.
- **The system messages on the right belonged to another character** — switching
  characters kept the old one's system log and whisper tabs on screen. Both now
  start clean for the character you just entered with, while zone changes,
  returns and reconnects keep the log you were reading.
- **Grab pets fetch loot from behind walls** — your rabbit, cat, monkey or gold
  pig used to run into a wall and give up when something you shot died on the
  other side, and the loot rotted where it fell. Pets now make their way to any
  drop within reach and bring it back.
- **Walking into the statues at the ancient Greek ruins** — the columns and
  statues in the God's Field ruins near Constantinople had a gap you could step
  through, leaving your character standing inside the stone with the camera
  buried in it. They are solid from every side now, and anyone already stuck
  inside one is pushed back out on their first step.
