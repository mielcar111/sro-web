## Added

- **A way out when you get stuck** — if the ground refuses to let you walk and you keep clicking, the game now offers a one-click Return to town. The free recall always worked from inside those spots; it just wasn't obvious it was there.
- **Sell straight from your bag** — hold Ctrl and click an item in your inventory while a merchant is open to put it up for sale, then press Enter to confirm. Dragging still works exactly as before, and one-of-a-kind gear still asks twice.

## Changed

- **More monsters, nearly everywhere** — spawn density is up by about 40% in every region. Camps that felt bare between kills should stay busy now.
- **The auto-hunt circle is a roaming area, not a fight range** — your character attacks any monster it can see, even well outside the circle, and the circle only decides where it wanders while there is nothing to fight. It can now be set as wide as 150.

## Fixed

- **Combos lose their damage after an imbue** — pressing an elemental imbue partway through a combo cancelled every strike that hadn't landed yet. The animation played out, the cooldown was spent, and the remaining hits did nothing. Combos now finish, imbue or not. This affected the Chinese combo families: the sword and spear chains, the Soul Spears, Crosswise and Flying Stone Smash, Devil and Demon Cut Blade, Dragon Sore Blade and both Arrow Combos.
- **"You are knocked down" when you were knocked back** — a shove that pushed you away held you still for a moment and then claimed you were on the floor. The message now says you were knocked back, and a real knockdown still says knocked down.
- **Skill effects fired off to the side** — cone and slash effects such as Wolf Bite Spear swept across the air in front of your character instead of into the enemy. They now point where the blow goes.
- **Auto-hunt stopped for no reason** — a brief connection hiccup looked like arriving in a new area, and silently ended the run. Short interruptions no longer stop it, and when it does stop because you genuinely changed area, it now tells you.
- **Auto-hunt ignored monsters that came after you** — a monster it had given up reaching, which then walked around the obstacle and started hitting you, stayed ignored for the next half minute while it stood there beating on you. Anything that reaches you is now fought back.
- **Auto-hunt silently skipped buffs that need another weapon** — a Cleric buff while you were holding a dagger was dropped for the whole run behind a vague line. It now names the weapon the buff needs and tells you to put that weapon in the buff list ahead of it.
- **Bandit quests only counted one kind of bandit** — Keep the Road Clear asked for Bandits, but the camp is mostly Bandit surbodinates, Bandit Archers and Bandit bowmen, and none of them counted. All four now do, so the quest is completable at the level it is offered. The Constantinople daily had the same problem and is fixed the same way.
- **Restless Stones ignored Tomb Stone Ghosts** — they share the graveyard with the Tomb Stones the quest asks for, and now count toward it.
- **Quest targets are easier to tell apart** — the quest log now shows the level of the monster you are hunting, so look-alikes sharing one camp — Tomb Stone and Tomb Stone Ghost, Stone Ghost and Broken Stone Ghost — are no longer guesswork.
- **European characters had someone else's face** — the portrait beside your health and mana, and in the party windows, showed the wrong head for every European look. Each one now shows your own.
- **The fields behind Jangan were empty** — the Water Ghost camp north of the city, and its neighbours, had been left out of the world entirely. Around 500 monsters are back where they belong.
- **A phantom stall after switching characters** — leaving a character with a stall open and logging in on another one could leave that character unable to move or cast, as though a stall it never opened was still running.
