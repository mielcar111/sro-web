## Added

- **Unique boss loot** — Tiger Girl, Cerberos, Captain Ivy, Uruchi, Isyutaru and
  Lord Yarkan now drop a proper kill burst: a shower of gold and items, a
  guaranteed equipment piece for the boss's level, and a 50% chance at a Seal
  item — bronze, silver or gold, with the better seals more likely on the
  stronger bosses.

## Changed

- **Hunt quest rewards** — gold rewards on all hunt quests are tripled, so
  clearing a quest now pays meaningfully better than farming the same monsters
  without it.

## Fixed

- **Chain combos keep all their hits** — pressing your combo key again while it
  was casting, or starting the combo while you were mid-swing, silently threw
  away the rest of the chain: 5 Arrow Combo often landed only 2 or 3 arrows,
  and sword chains stopped after 2 hits. Combos now always play out in full
  unless you deliberately press a different skill to cancel into it.
- **Unique bosses dropped nothing** — high-level hunters got no loot at all
  from the unique bosses because of the low-level-monster loot rule. Uniques
  are now always worth killing regardless of your level.
- **Invisible buff casts** — the Heuksal spin buffs (Bloody Fan Storm and its
  line) and the Bicheon shield buffs (Castle Shield and its line) never played
  their casting animation; your character now performs the cast motion.
- **Skills learned mid-session** — the very first cast of a freshly learned
  skill could play no animation; it now animates from the first press.
