## Added

- **Strap** — the Dark Mentalist passive can now be learned. It works exactly as in the original game: your damage-over-time curses (Blaze, Toxin, Decayed, Curse Breath lines) last longer at each rank, landing up to three extra hits. It no longer asks for a Dark Staff to learn.

## Changed

- **Curses land instantly** — Blaze, Toxin, Decayed and Curse Breath now deal their damage, roll their affliction and start their damage-over-time the moment the cast finishes, matching the original timing.
- **Vampire Touch & Vampire Kiss** — the life drain now follows the original formula: your equipped weapon's magical power and your Intelligence increase the amount, targets far above your level resist part of it, and you are healed for the full damage dealt to each victim. Vampire Kiss's additional victims now take reduced amounts the further down the chain they are, and a low-health victim can still lose all remaining health at once.
- **Shadow Armor & Dusk Armor** — the damaging shield now follows the original's rhythm: it strikes the moment you cast it and roughly every two seconds after, for its full sixteen seconds, hitting up to five nearby enemies with decreasing damage per additional target. In PvP areas it also hits hostile players.
- **Afflictions follow the original resistance rules** — Daze, Slumber, Fog Sight, Wheel Bind, the disease from the Vampire line and all four Raze curses now use the original chance and duration rules: targets far above the skill's grade resist more often and recover sooner, with the original minimums. The matching Chinese skills (Fortify Cancel and the Chain Sword curse) follow the same rules.

## Fixed

- **Skill tooltips** — life-drain skills no longer claim to return a percentage of the damage; they now correctly say the damage dealt is absorbed as HP.
