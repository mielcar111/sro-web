## Added

- **Your pets come back with you** — a pet that was still out when you logged off is summoned again the moment you enter the world, exactly like the original game. Your grab settings come back with it. A pet you unsummoned on purpose stays unsummoned.
- **Teleport back to where you died** — the Teleport action now asks where you want to go first: a spot on the map, "Where you died", or "Where you returned from". Picking "Where you died" takes you straight back to the spot you fell. It stays available until your next death, and works across regions.
- **Teleport back to where you returned from** — the same menu offers "Where you returned from", the spot you were standing when your last Return finished. Handy for stepping into town and going straight back to your hunting spot. Both entries show the region and how long ago you left it, and stay greyed out until you have one recorded.
- **Ultrawide resolution** — 3440x1440 is now available in Settings for players on ultrawide monitors.
- **Magic stones stack** — magic stones now pile up to 50 in a slot instead of eating one slot each. Stones only share a slot when they carry the same assimilation risk, so a batch of freshly crafted stones lands as a handful of piles rather than a full bag.
- **Risk in the stone's name** — a magic stone's assimilation risk is now part of its name, for example "Magic Stone of Strength (D1) (10%)". That is how you tell your 10% pile from your 30% pile at a glance. Luck, Astral and Preservation stones carry no risk and stack together as one pile.

## Fixed

- **Giants no longer look like uniques on the minimap** — the purple dot is the unique marker again. Champions and giants share the ordinary red dot, so a purple dot on your minimap means a real unique is nearby.
- **Right-click belongs to the game** — right-clicking anywhere in the game window no longer pops up the browser's own menu over the top of it.
- **No more accidental text highlighting** — clicking and dragging across the interface no longer paints windows and labels blue. Chat text can still be selected and copied.
- **Buffs no longer hide behind the party list** — your buff icons now run along the top of the screen next to your character frame instead of stacking down into the party frames, so a full set of buffs stays readable while you're in a party.
- **Teleport said nothing while on cooldown** — using the Teleport action too soon after the last one did nothing at all, with no explanation. It now tells you how many seconds are left.
- **Traded magic stones lost their risk** — a magic stone that changed hands through a trade or a personal shop arrived with its assimilation risk stripped off, turning it into a stone that could never assimilate. Stones now keep the risk they were crafted with, wherever they travel.
- **Sliding characters when you enter the world** — characters could appear and glide around with no walking animation for a moment after entering the world or coming into view. Everyone now arrives already animated, in the right stance for what they're doing — and the monsters standing around you now load in with the world instead of sliding around stiffly for the first few seconds.
- **Stuck "on horse" after reconnecting** — reconnecting in the same tab could leave you looking mounted when you weren't, greying out every skill and blocking the hunting macro. Your mount state is now restored correctly on login, and a single Dismount click clears any leftover state.
- **Verification links stay valid** — clicking "Send the email again" no longer breaks the links from earlier emails. Every link we've sent you works until it expires on its own, so it doesn't matter which email you open.
- **Clicking a link twice** — opening a verification link again (a refresh, a second device, or an overeager mail app) now shows "your email is already verified" instead of a confusing "invalid link" error.
- **Turkish and German players lost their links** — if you had switched the site to Türkçe or Deutsch, verification and password-reset links from your emails landed on a broken page every single time. That's fixed — the links now survive the language switch.
- **Wizards froze before every staff attack** — normal attacks with a two-handed staff, wand or rod left the character standing still for about a second before anything happened. The charge-up animation now starts the instant the attack does — and other players see it too.
- **Fire Blow's flame vanished early** — the flame cone of Fire Blow and Salamander Blow disappeared a couple of seconds in while the skill kept going; it now burns for the skill's whole duration.
- **Meteor falls from the sky again** — Meteor's rock now drops onto the target instead of appearing on the ground, and the falling shards of Snow Storm - Ice rain and Flame Wave - Wide rain down as well.
- **Teleport plays its real animation** — Teleport and Aerial Teleport now raise the staff like the original instead of playing a bare-handed cast.
- **Skill tooltips no longer show raw values** — some effects appeared as code, for example Life Control's "-0.5 maxHpPct". They now read plainly: Life Control and Life Turnover show "-50% maximum HP", and the STR and INT bonuses, MP recovery, healing power and MP cost lines of other skills are written out the same way.

## Changed

- **The world map remembers your view** — reopening the map brings back the zoom and position you left it at, instead of resetting to the whole world every time.
- **Emails in your language, links to your language** — verification and password-reset emails now open the site in the same language as the email, instead of always dropping you on the English page.
- **Registering while signed in** — the create-account page now tells you when you're already signed in and points you to your account page, so a lost verification email doesn't trick you into making a second account by mistake. You can still sign out and create another account from right there.
- **Multi-hit skills hit at full strength** — Frozen Spear, Charged Wind and Charged Squall — and the multi-hit chains of other masteries such as 4 Arrow Combo, 5 Arrow Combo, Cunning Stab and Butterfly Blow — now deal the original full damage on every hit instead of splitting one hit's damage across the volley. All of them hit noticeably harder, and the hits of each stage land together in their original bursts.
- **Area damage falls off like the original** — an area skill still hits at most its original number of targets (five for most, three for Meteor), and each additional target now takes progressively less damage than the one before instead of the full amount. Ground Charge, Ground Rave and Charged Squall pick their extra targets around the caster, exactly as the original does.
- **Fire Blow, Salamander Blow, Lightning Bolt and Chain Lightning** — the flame sprays and the lightning now follow their original target rules too: up to three targets, with reduced damage on each additional one.
- **Teleport reaches its original distance** — Teleport now jumps 10/12.5/15 meters by rank and Aerial Teleport 18 meters; it previously jumped far beyond the original range. It also lands exactly on the spot you clicked, including when it was queued during another action.
- **Intelligence saves exactly the right mana** — the Intelligence passive's discount is now applied precisely the way the original computes it; many wizard skills cost a little less MP.
- **Freeze, Frostbite, Electric Shock, Root, Fear and Mana Drain** — these effects now use the original formulas: durations come from the skill's effect level against the target's resistance, and the slows of Frostbite and Dull combine the original way.
- **Earth Barrier and Earth Fence** — now reduce physical damage only, exactly like the original (magic passes through), cover you plus up to seven party members, and casting one no longer cancels the other — an active barrier stays until it runs out, and only a higher rank of the same skill replaces it.
- **Earth, Cold, Fire and Lightning Spirit empower like the original** — the four Spirit passives now multiply your magical attack power on their element's skills (+4% per rank, +80% at rank 20) instead of adding a small fixed amount of damage. Wizard element skills hit far harder with the Spirits learned — this is the original formula.
