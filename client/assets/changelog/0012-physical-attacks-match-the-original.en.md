## Changed

- **Physical damage is now computed the way the original game computes it** — the original keeps the attack number shown on your character window separate from the attack number used inside combat, and this server had been mixing the two. Untangling them corrects physical damage across the board: European Warrior and Rogue attacks lose a mastery boost they were never meant to have (their weapon attack had been quietly growing with mastery level — a sizable unearned advantage at high mastery), while Chinese sword, spear and bow attacks come out slightly stronger, because the original multiplies the whole attack — weapon and skill together — by your mastery, at exactly 1% per level.
- **Sprint Assault stays strong** — it is the one European attack that genuinely grows with Warrior mastery in the original, and it deals the same damage as before; it is simply computed correctly now.
- **The character window follows the original too** — with a Chinese weapon, a staff, a dark staff or a rod equipped, the physical attack line shows your mastery-boosted value exactly as the original displays it; with a European sword, axe, dagger, crossbow or harp it shows the plain weapon value. This is display only — staff users' physical attack number goes up on the page without changing any hit, and European melee pages come down to the value their hits were always meant to use.
- **Mastery counts the moment a hit lands** — if your mastery goes up while a slow weapon (crossbow, wand, staff, rod) is still winding up its swing, the landed hit now uses your new mastery instead of the value from when the swing started.
- **Everything else already corrected stays put** — Chinese skills and imbues, the staff/rod/dark-staff normal attacks, the Wizard Spirit passives and all European skill mechanics keep working exactly as in the last updates.
