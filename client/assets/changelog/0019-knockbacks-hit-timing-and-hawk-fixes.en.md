## Fixed

- **Knockbacks no longer knock enemies to the ground** — a knockback is a shove: the target slides away and is briefly staggered, but stays on its feet, as in the original. Only true knockdown skills put an enemy on the floor. Power Shot, Intense Shot, Shield Trash, Shield Crush, Bash, Maddening, Dare Devil, Sprint Assault, the Ghost Spears, 5 Arrow Combo and Charged Wind/Squall all pushed *and* floored their targets before. Blast Shot, Hurricane Shot, Turn Rising and the Blood/Soul/Demon Blade Forces are the real knockdowns and are unchanged — they still shove the enemy back as they fall.
- **Damage numbers wait for the blow to land** — Slash, Horror Chord, Weird Chord, Spinning, Wounds and a dozen other skills showed their damage the instant you pressed the key, while the weapon was still winding up. The number and the impact now appear when the strike actually connects. Multi-hit skills show one number per blow, spread across the swing.
- **Skill tooltips said "party member" when any ally could receive the skill** — Physical and Magical Fence, Protect, Physical Screen, Morale Screen, Force and Mental Blessing, Force and Mental Deity, Mana Switch, Mana Wind, Duplicate and Scream Mask can all be cast on any ally. The tooltip was really trying to say "you cannot cast this on yourself", which is now its own line. Pain Quota is the one skill that genuinely needs a party member, and it says so.
- **Bard dances now tell you they need a song** — the six dances only work inside another bard's song, but the tooltip never mentioned it, so the skill just refused to cast. The requirement is now listed with the harp requirement.
- **Blue Hawk and Lightning Hawk fly again** — both summons hovered motionless over the archer instead of flapping. Lightning Hawk was also showing the Blue Hawk's plumage; it now has its own.
- **Snow Storm - Ice Rain falls like rain** — the ice shards flew in at an angle from the caster. They now drop straight down onto the target area, as in the original.
- **Poison Weapons sits on the hand** — with a dagger equipped, the poison glow on the left hand floated above the fist instead of coating it. The Chinese elemental imbues had the same problem on that hand.
- **Blessing connection limits are clearer** — Force Blessing and Force Deity share one pool of connections between them (as do Mental Blessing and Mental Deity), so filling up on one leaves no room for the other. The tooltip now names the skill it shares with instead of letting it look like a bug.
