## Added

- **Scorn & Gross Scorn** — the Rogue's PvP taunts can now be learned and used. As in the original game, a mocked player can attack only you while the scorn lasts; Gross Scorn catches up to three players around your target. The effect ends early if the mocker falls.

## Changed

- **Crossbow Power Up & Dagger Power Up** — the two weapon passives now raise the attack strength your hits are rolled from by a percentage, as in the original — up to +80% at the highest rank — instead of adding a tiny fixed amount. The bonus now applies to normal attacks too, not only skills. Enemy defense still counts afterwards, so the final numbers grow by less than the raw percentage. The Warrior passives One-Handed Arms, Two-Handed Arms and Dual Arms received the same correction.
- **Hawk Eye** — the passive now truly works: your dagger hits land closer to their maximum damage, exactly the way the original applies it. The Warrior's Accuracy passive follows the same rule on dual axes.
- **Wide Sight** — now also extends the reach of your normal crossbow attacks, not just skills.
- **Venom Extension** — the passive now actually works: it extends how long your Poison Weapons and Vein Weapon coating stays on your blade, up to 8.5 seconds at the highest rank (from the base 6). The poison it inflicts on enemies is unchanged.
- **Chained attacks step by step** — Intense Shot, Hurricane Shot, Combo Blow, Mortal Wounds and Butterfly Blow now play out as in the original: every follow-up blow is its own confirmed step with its own timing and recovery instead of one long merged action, and an interrupted chain simply stops. Hurricane Shot's second bolt can now knock enemies down, and the knocked-down time matches the original.
- **Normal dagger attacks hit harder** — both blows of the dagger's double swing now carry the full attack value, as in the original, instead of splitting it between them. The two-packet swings of other weapons follow the same rule.
- **Shot ranges restored** — Fast Shot and Rapid Shot now reach much further, and Long Shot and Distance Shot further still beyond the crossbow's own range, matching their original distances. After these four shots your character no longer swings automatically — also as in the original; other attack skills keep flowing into normal attacks.
- **No more dancing around small enemies** — melee attacks and skills now connect body-to-body, so a small monster circling just outside your dagger's reach no longer causes an endless chase.
- **Screw** — the strike now lands the instant the swing fires instead of a moment later.
- **Screw & Prick stuns** — stun chances now follow the original's resistance rules much more closely: enemies far above your skill's grade resist more often and recover sooner. A small part of the original's admission rules is still being reconstructed.
- **Down attacks** — the ×1.5 bonus of skills like Power Shot, Intense Shot, Wounds and Mortal Wounds now triggers only against enemies lying on the ground, not against ones merely pushed back. The Warrior's stab and counter bonus is separate and now triggers against sleeping, bound, dulled or stunned enemies — as in the original — rather than downed ones.
- **Avoid** — now reduces exactly the attacks the original counts as ranged physical hits, no more and no fewer.
- **Attack buffs vs. armor** — Crossbow Extreme, Dagger Desperate and similar attack-raising buffs are now applied after the enemy's defense, as in the original; against heavily armored enemies they help a little less than before.
- **Scud** — speed boosts no longer stack: the most recent one applies, exactly like the original's single haste slot.
- **Poison Field** — the cloud always poisoned enemies all around you; it now also picks its up-to-five victims the way the original does instead of simply taking the nearest ones.
- **Knockdowns and knockbacks are exclusive** — an enemy already on the ground cannot be knocked down again, a knockback replaces a knockdown (and the other way round), and pushed enemies now stop cleanly at walls instead of sliding along them.
- **Tag Point disabled** — the tracking skill is switched off for now, alongside the stealth and mask lines; its map-tracking system is not part of the game yet.
